# Live Demo Walkthrough Script

Use this exact script for your **live judging demonstration** and your **2-minute backup video recording**.

---

## Preparation (Before Starting)
1. Have your Nonilion room open (`www.nonilion.com`) with the room named **"Strategy War Room"**.
2. Ensure **OmniChief** is imported and visible in the room.
3. Open screen recording software (OBS, Windows Snipping Tool, or Loom) for the backup video.

---

## Scene 1: The Main Showcase (Multi-Format Deliverables)

### What to Type in Nonilion Chat:
> **"OmniChief, we are deciding between 3 customer acquisition channels for our B2B SaaS product: LinkedIn InMail, Cold Email Automation, and Google Search Ads with a $15,000 monthly budget. Analyze CAC, conversion timelines, and provide an executive allocation plan with a chart and downloadable CSV data."**

### What to Point Out to Judges:
1. **Executive Planning:** Point out that OmniChief started with a clear 3-step action plan, not generic advice.
2. **Decision Matrix:** Highlight the Markdown comparison table showing Blended CAC (\$214.29) and timeline breakdowns.
3. **Live Visual Chart:** Point out the rendered chart image directly embedded in the chat panel.
4. **Data Artifact:** Point out the copyable/downloadable CSV code block ready for spreadsheet import.
5. **Team Delegation:** Note how it autonomously assigns follow-ups to specific team roles in the office room.

---

## Scene 2: Cognitive Guardrails (Edge-Case Handling)

Judges test requirement #5: *"It handles an unclear or impossible request sensibly: it asks a follow-up question or explains what it cannot do, instead of failing silently or making things up."*

### What to Type in Nonilion Chat:
> **"OmniChief, make our startup profitable by tomorrow with $0 marketing budget."**

### What to Point Out to Judges:
1. OmniChief **does not fail silently** and **does not hallucinate** an unrealistic promise.
2. It detects the hyper-compressed timeline and zero-budget constraint.
3. It asks 2–3 precise diagnostic questions (retention rate, current MRR, organic referral baseline).
4. It offers a realistic immediate alternative (48-hour organic customer reactivation sprint).

---

## Scene 3: Office Voice & Collaboration (Optional Bonus)
